# Experimental interpretation and scope

The experimental analyses associated with the current Kiknadze–Krasnov project answer three different questions. They should not be interpreted as a single ranking of laboratory conditions.

## 1. Direct local zero-crossing test

A direct analysis of condition V1 compares a reconstructed zero of the mean radial velocity with a positive-radius zero of the radial derivative of the measured axial vorticity, equivalently the zero of the modeled radial diffusive-vorticity flux `-nu ∂_r omega_parallel`.

This is a local spatial consistency test of the common-zero geometry. It does not identify a source parameter and does not test material conservation in time.

## 2. Fit-based all-condition common-zero mining

A separate analysis applies common fitted estimators across the eight BCO-DMO conditions. Under that pipeline, H2 is the strongest resolved positive-radius candidate and V3 is a secondary, more resolution-limited candidate.

The stagnation radius in this analysis is obtained from a fitted axisymmetric mean-field radial model, and the vorticity-extremum radius is obtained from a fitted vorticity profile. These estimators are therefore not identical to the direct V1 zero-crossing estimator.

## 3. Global KK-versus-Burgers/source identification

A third analysis asks whether the full resolved radial profiles identify a quantitatively self-consistent source-bearing KK state. The archive remains predominantly Burgers-like at resolved scales. The strongest generalized-profile indication is not sufficient to establish a positive source parameter consistently across the independent radial-velocity and vorticity-shape observables.

## Reconciliation

The three analyses are complementary:

- the direct V1 test probes local zero co-location;
- the H2/V3 analysis probes resolved fitted common-zero candidates across conditions;
- the global analysis probes source-parameter identification from the resolved profiles.

A condition may therefore be informative under one test and not under another without logical contradiction.

## Formal-certification boundary

The Lean development proves statements about the exact KK family under explicit mathematical and physical premises. The laboratory archive is not a premise of those proofs. In particular, the experiments do not directly establish:

- time-dependent materiality of the distinguished cylinder;
- exact conservation of the enclosed material circulation;
- the exponential scaled-coordinate selection law;
- a quantitatively self-consistent positive source parameter for the measured laboratory field.

Those distinctions should be preserved in any manuscript, presentation, or downstream analysis using this repository.
